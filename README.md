# Dashboard_of_sales_sample
Overview
This dashboard provides a comprehensive visualization of sales performance data for a business, likely based on a sample dataset (e.g., similar to the Superstore sales dataset). It includes key performance indicators (KPIs) and various charts to analyze sales, quantity, profit, and discounts across categories, sub-categories, states, and time periods. The dashboard is designed for quick insights into regional performance, product profitability, and temporal trends.
The dashboard appears to be built using Microsoft Power BI, as indicated by the footer branding.
Key Metrics
The top section displays aggregated KPIs:

Total Sum of Sales: 2.30M (total revenue generated)
Sum of Quantity: 38K (total units sold)
Sum of Profit: 286.40K (net profit after costs)
Sum of Discount: 1.56K (total discounts applied)

These metrics provide an at-a-glance summary of overall performance.
Visualizations
The dashboard includes the following interactive visuals for deeper analysis:

Bubble Chart: Sum of Quantity and Sum of Sales by Sub-Category
Description: A scatter plot with bubbles representing sub-categories (e.g., Machines, Copiers, Binders, Paper, Furnishings, Phones, Storage, Art, Accessories, Envelopes, Labels, Tables, Fasteners, Supplies, Bookcases, Chairs, Appliances).
Axes: X-axis shows Sum of Sales; Y-axis shows Sum of Quantity.
Purpose: Identifies high-volume or high-value sub-categories. Larger bubbles indicate higher combined impact.

Horizontal Bar Chart: Sum of Profit by Sub-Category
Description: Bars ranked by profit for sub-categories (e.g., Phones, Chairs, Accessories, Storage, Binders, Paper, Furnishings, etc.).
Axes: Y-axis lists sub-categories; X-axis shows profit in thousands (0K to 50K).
Purpose: Highlights the most and least profitable product sub-categories.

Line Chart: Sum of Sales by Order Date
Description: A time-series line graph showing sales fluctuations over order dates.
Axes: X-axis represents order dates (numerically labeled from ~4300 to 4500, possibly representing date codes or IDs); Y-axis shows sales in thousands (0K to 30K).
Purpose: Tracks sales trends over time, useful for identifying peaks, troughs, or seasonal patterns.

Donut Chart: Sum of Sales by Category and Sub-Category
Description: A circular chart dividing sales by major categories (e.g., Technology, Office Supplies, Furniture) and further into sub-categories (e.g., Phones, Chairs, Bookcases).
Purpose: Shows the proportional contribution of each category and sub-category to total sales.

Word Cloud: Sum of Profit by State
Description: A tag cloud where state names (e.g., California, New York, Washington, Michigan, Virginia, Texas, Florida, Illinois, etc.) are sized based on profit contribution. Larger fonts indicate higher profits (e.g., California and New York are prominent).
Purpose: Quickly visualizes profit distribution across U.S. states, emphasizing top-performing regions.

Bubble Map: Sum of Sales by State
Description: A geographic map of the United States with bubbles overlaid on states. Bubble size corresponds to sales volume.
Purpose: Provides a spatial view of sales performance, highlighting regional hotspots (e.g., larger bubbles in California, New York, and Texas).
